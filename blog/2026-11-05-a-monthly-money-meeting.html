Money management

A monthly money meeting that takes less than an hour

November 5, 2026 · Mark Heeney, Mortgage Agent Level 2

Most money stress is not a missing spreadsheet. It is a month that ended before anyone looked at what actually left the account. A monthly money meeting is a fixed hour, on a calendar, where the household reads the numbers out loud and picks one change for the next 30 days.

Why this matters now

Renewals, car repairs, and property tax do not wait for a perfect budget. People in Windsor and across Ontario often know the mortgage payment and still get surprised by everything around it. One meeting a month catches the drift while it is still small.

A practical checklist

  • Pick a date that already exists, such as the day after payday or the first Sunday of the month.
  • Open the chequing account, the credit cards, and the mortgage or rent line. Write the balances, not a story about them.
  • List bills due in the next 30 days and any annual bill hiding in that month.
  • Name one leak: a subscription, a category that ran hot, or a minimum payment that is not moving the balance.
  • Choose one action only. Extra actions get abandoned by the next payday.

Common mistakes

  • Turning the hour into a fight about last month instead of a plan for this one.
  • Building a new app or spreadsheet before the same five numbers have been written down twice.
  • Skipping the meeting in a good month. The habit is the point.

Where a mortgage conversation fits

If the meeting keeps showing that high-interest balances are crowding the mortgage, or that a renewal payment would break the month, that is the moment to talk it through with a mortgage agent. The meeting does not approve anything. It makes the file clearer.
